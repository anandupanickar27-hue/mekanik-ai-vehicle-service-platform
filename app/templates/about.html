About Mekanik

Mekanik is an AI-assisted vehicle service platform that helps vehicle owners diagnose problems, connect with qualified mechanics, and manage vehicle servicing efficiently.

🎯 Why Mekanik?

Vehicle owners often struggle to identify issues and choose the right mechanic. Mekanik combines Artificial Intelligence with a modern service management system to simplify diagnosis, appointment booking, and repair tracking.

🚗 Customer Guide

As a customer, Mekanik helps you identify vehicle issues, discover trusted mechanics, and manage the complete servicing process from one place.

Try These Features
  1. Add one or more vehicles.
  2. Select a vehicle in the AI Assistant.
  3. Describe your vehicle issue.
  4. Receive an AI-powered diagnosis.
  5. Browse recommended mechanics.
  6. Book a service appointment.
  7. Track appointment progress.
  8. Leave a review after service.

👨‍🔧 Mechanic Guide

As a mechanic, Mekanik helps you manage appointments, update repair progress, and build your professional reputation.
